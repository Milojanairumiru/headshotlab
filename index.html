SMARTPHONE PHOTO GUIDE

RULE 01

LIGHTING

Face a large window with soft indirect daylight. Avoid harsh direct sun casting deep shadows under your nose or eyes.

  • Even, diffused daylight
  • No harsh overhead yellow lamps
RULE 02

CAMERA DISTANCE

Hold the camera at eye level, roughly 3 to 5 feet away. Use 2x optical zoom if available to prevent wide-angle distortion.

  • Eye-level alignment
  • Chest-up framing
RULE 03

BACKGROUND

Any neutral wall or quiet indoor space works well. We will cleanly replace the backdrop with our editorial studio tones.

  • Plain wall or open room
  • No cluttered objects behind head
RULE 04

POSTURE & ANGLE

Angle your shoulders 15-20 degrees away from the lens and turn your face gently toward the camera for a dynamic stance.

  • Relaxed shoulders
  • Slight diagonal angle
RULE 05

EXPRESSION

A natural, confident smile with eyes engaged. Think of a positive conversation rather than a stiff passport freeze.

  • Approachable & authentic
  • Relaxed jawline
RULE 06

WARDROBE

Solid neutral colors, structured collars, blazers, or clean crew necks. Avoid busy small patterns that can cause moiré.

  • Solid colors (navy, charcoal, ivory)
  • Clean, unwrinkled fabric

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT KIND OF PHOTO SHOULD I UPLOAD?
+

Any well-lit smartphone photo taken in natural daylight (facing a window) works wonders. Even a clear selfie or casual portrait is suitable as long as your facial features are in focus and not blurry.

HOW LONG DOES DELIVERY TAKE?
+

Individual orders are delivered to your email within 48 hours. Team orders generally take 24 to 72 hours depending on the roster size.

WILL MY FACE LOOK LIKE A FAKE AI FILTER?
+

Absolutely not. We take great pride in our Anti-Filter policy. We do not generate hallucinations or replace your facial structure. Every image is refined by professional photo retouchers to ensure high-fidelity realism.

WHAT IF I NEED A REVISION?
+

All individual orders come with 2 free revision rounds, and team orders come with 3 rounds. If you want any adjustments to lighting, wardrobe colors, or background tones, just reply to your delivery email!

DO YOU OFFER BULK DISCOUNTS FOR COMPANIES?
+

Yes! We offer tiered volume pricing for teams of 5 to 500+ employees. You will receive a unified brand guide, a private team upload link, and invoice billing.
